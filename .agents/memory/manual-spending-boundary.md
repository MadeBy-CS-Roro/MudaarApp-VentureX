---
name: Manual spending boundary
description: Why manually entered spending must remain separate from bank-derived budget calibration.
---

Manual expense entry is for flexible spending, not evidence of income or usual essential expenses. Keep manual descriptions and merchant names out of salary detection and historical essentials calibration, even when adding richer expense features later.

**Why:** Free-form manual descriptions can resemble payroll or essential-store records. Treating them as bank evidence would change the spending allowance itself rather than simply recording spending against it.

**How to apply:** Preserve that distinction when extending classification, editing, importing, or history features. A manual entry's selected flexible category should remain independent of bank merchant corrections.
