---
name: Prepayment quote semantics
description: Why the pay-all quote differs from the gross amount for multi-payment plans.
---

The pay-all quote is the additional amount beyond the installment already reserved in this month's obligations. Do not equate "reserved" with evidence that a bank payment has already happened.

**Why:** The continuation requires Tamara's quote to be 600 even though detection reports two remaining 600 installments. One is already covered by the monthly budget, leaving 600 additional. A due-date-based paid/unpaid heuristic alone cannot reconcile that requirement.

**How to apply:** Show the additional quote, reserved portion, and gross settlement clearly in confirmation. Recording settlement is not a bank transfer. Preserve the current reservation, deduct the additional prepayment from current capacity, and release future installments; never create spending capacity by deleting an already-reserved payment.
